    Welcome to Chain Reaction!

    You and your opponents take turns placing atoms on the grid, one atom per turn. On your first turn, you can place anywhere. After that, you can only place on cells you already own.

    Every cell can only hold so many atoms before it explodes. How many? Simple: one for each neighbour it has.

    • A corner cell explodes after 2 atoms
    • An edge cell explodes after 3 atoms
    • A middle cell explodes after 4 atoms

    When a cell explodes, it fires one atom into each neighbour. If your explosion hits an opponent's cell, you steal it. Their atoms become yours. And if that neighbour was already nearly full? It explodes too, setting off a CHAIN REACTION.

    One cell blows, triggers another, triggers another. Watch the whole board flip in a single move.

    A player is eliminated the moment they run out of atoms on the board.

    Last one standing wins.
